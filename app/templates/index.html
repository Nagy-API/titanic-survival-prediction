Model evaluation

How the estimate was built

A stratified split reserved a portion of the historical sample for evaluation. Five-fold cross-validation on the training portion compared a majority-class baseline, Logistic Regression and Random Forest. Each fold learned imputation, encoding and scaling from its own training data.
